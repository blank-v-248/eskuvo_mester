You can let us know via this Google Form.

Accomandation?

There is accommodation on site for around 100 guests. Please indicate your accommodation needs in the RSVP form. Breakfast the next morning is included in the price. If you don’t plan to stay on site, we kindly ask you to arrange your accommodation and transportation there individually.

Gifts – what do we like?

Your presence is the most important gift for us. In keeping with local wedding traditions, guests often give a financial gift in an envelope, symbolically contributing to the cost of the celebration. This would be warmly and gratefully received, however is not expected - as said, celebrating together is the best present.
